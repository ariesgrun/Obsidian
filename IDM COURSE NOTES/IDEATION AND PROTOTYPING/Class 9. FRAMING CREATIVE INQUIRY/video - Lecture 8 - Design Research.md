
- methodology is larger ideological framework, through which project is created
- human centered design - people you are desiging for and ends with new solutions that are tailor made to suit their needs (very iterative)
	- may lead to oversimplification of complex problems, or overvalue technical solutions
	- FEEDBACK is important
- design thinking - provide generic framework and its applicable to all areas of expertise of your company and on hte market
	- empathy, define, ideate, prototype, test
- participatory design
	- involve all stakeholders in the design process
- systems thinking
	- make sense of a complex system thatgives attention to exploring the relationships, boundaries, and perspectives in a system
- speculative design 
	- use design to create ideas (possible futures), the what if, to open debate/discussion about he kind of future people want or dont want
- critical design 
	- challenging narrow assumptions, preconceptions about the role products play in everyday life. defines an attitude/position
