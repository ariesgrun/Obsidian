# Everyone is a creator

- creativity is not a rare ability/ or difficult to access
	- our birthright
- to create is to bring someting into existence that wan't there before.
	- could be a conversation, the solution to a problem, a note to a friend/arrangement of furniture
- What you make doesn’t have to be witnessed, recorded, sold, or encased in glass for it to be a work of art
- We create forests and oceans, warmth and cold. We read words, hear voices, and form interpretations. Then, in an instant, we produce a response. ( All of this in a world of our own creation)
- we are all living as artists
- we perceive, filter, and collect data, then curate an experience for ourselves and others based on this information set
- by the mere fact of being alive, we are active participants in the ongoing process of creation
- to perceive, refine to the subtle, noticing your life is a form of self expression, a work of art
