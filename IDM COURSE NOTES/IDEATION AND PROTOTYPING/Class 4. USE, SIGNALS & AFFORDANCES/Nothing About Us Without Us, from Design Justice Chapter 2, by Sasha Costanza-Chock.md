Participatory design is a collaborative approach that actively involves end-users, stakeholders, and workers directly in the creation and decision-making processes of products, services, or environments.

Another lesson from disability activism is that involving members of the community that is most directly affected by a design process is crucial, both because justice demands it and also because the tacit and experiential knowledge of community members is sure to produce ideas, approaches, and innovations  that a nonmember of the community would be extremely unlikely to come up with.


Even when there is a clear need for a new tech solution, community-specific user research should precede design and development.
