- develop your own artistic research

Eugenia Zurowski;s Where Do You Know From (2020)
Notes
	- western culture has consistently privileged the spoken word as the highest form of intellectual practice and seen visual representations as second rate illustrations of ideas
	- art and design can functions not only as subjects/topics of research, but methods for conducting this same research, as well as tools and media for communication its findings
	- research - range of activities that support original and innovative work in the whole range of academic, professional, and technological fields
		- in context of art - broad range of methods and coming different forms of knowing
	- objective of research document - share knowledge that has been developed through research
	- will become better readers of texts/images, better judges of concepts/ideas/better communicators of their own work
	- by sharing knowledge you participate and help in building a community that is continually in the making

Why make a research document
- 2500-12000 words
- usually produced in order to obtain degree
- gain experience
	- structuring complex/diverse materials
- period of time and focused attention in which you can follow your own interests seriously and rigorously, under the guidance of a professional advisor and embedded within a larger research community
- provides basis for artistic project.
	- improves your practice.
	- share knowledge for wider audiences
